Gold impact — general relationship

  • Dovish Fed / rate cuts expected → typically supportive of Gold (lower opportunity cost, weaker dollar bias).
  • Hawkish Fed / rate hikes or "higher for longer" → typically pressures Gold.
  • These are general tendencies, not rules — price action confirmation on the chart always matters more than the macro read alone.
